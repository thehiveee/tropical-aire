Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Tropical Aire is committed to making our website usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA colour-contrast ratios.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link.
  • Images have descriptive alt text, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have labels and clear, announced error messages.
  • Animations are turned off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Need help?

If anything on this site is hard to use, call (718) 766-0889 or email William@tropical-aire.com and we'll help you directly and fix the issue.
